Where AI adoption gets stuck.

There's a lot of AI activity inside most organizations right now, but most of it stays fragmented. Pilots get launched and prove out something useful, but they never get embedded in how people work day to day. Different teams end up running variations of the same experiment without knowing about each other. Middle managers are being asked to evaluate AI-assisted work without clear standards. And by the time an AI policy is approved, parts of it are already out of date.

In my experience, most of the harder work in AI adoption sits on the organizational side: decision rights, governance, workflow redesign, evaluation in practice, how learning moves between teams. Vision and pilots are the easier part. The operating model is where it gets stuck.

That's the layer where I work. Senior advisory on strategy, governance, operating model design, and the capacity to use AI well.

The AI Transformation Canvas.

The AI Transformation Canvas is a strategic design tool for leadership teams. It organizes the AI transition into eight areas of strategy and action across three layers: strategic focus, adaptation, and enablement. Teams use it in working sessions to diagnose where the organization is, ideate about what could change, and plan what comes next.

How to work together.

There are a few ways to get started, from a single working session to multi-month advisory engagements. The best place to start is a 30-minute conversation about what you're working through and what would be most useful.

Briefing session

60–90 minutes · Leadership or Working Team

A working session for thinking through a specific question together. Could be a Canvas-based map of where the organization is, an orientation on AI governance, AI in service and experience design, or another topic. The team leaves with a shared view and a clearer sense of what to focus on next.

Workshop

Half-day to Two-day · Leadership, Strategy, or Working Group

A longer working session for going deeper into a specific area. Could be a full Canvas walkthrough, AI governance and decision-making, AI in service and experience design, or another area. The team leaves with a synthesis and a sequenced plan with named owners.

Advisory engagement

Multi-week or multi-month · Organization or Team-based Engagement

Working with leadership on the AI transition, focused on the harder decisions and coordination as the organization moves. Common shapes include a transition assessment (4–6 weeks), an operating model design (8–12 weeks), or a senior advisory retainer (3–6 months).
